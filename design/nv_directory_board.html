Nevada directory: design options

Critique of the PR 2 build against your tenets, then side-by-side options with real players from the 2026 list. Pick per section; nothing here is locked in.

1 · What's off today

Doesn't look like the rest of the site
  • Blue-gray header band, bordered table card, uppercase headers, native dropdowns.
  • The division dashboards use warm cards, pill tabs, and plain tables.
Wireframey lines and wasted width
  • A rule under every row plus a box around the table.
  • Table stretches to 1100px for six short columns, so Name sits 480px from its rating.
Numbers not aligned
  • 2026 and 2027 are left-aligned text.
  • Rating letter has a dotted underline that reads as a link.
Copy clutter
  • Two-line subtitle repeats what the columns say.
  • Seven-item rating-type legend under the table; a hover on the letter is enough.
ShiQ is blank for 81% of rows
  • Only 428 of 2,299 players have match history, so the column looks broken at first glance.
  • Staying blank (decided: no "Has matches" filter).
Mobile
  • Filters wrap to four rows; Δ, 2027, and ShiQ are off-screen to the right.
  • Player page: footnote under the table, cards with borders, all/2026 toggle shows the same thing twice.
